# Data

## `raw/` — original files, unmodified

Weyrauch, A., Taubert, O., Götz, E., Streit, A., Götz, M., Debus, C., & Wolter, F. (2024).
*Hourly Water Demand of a Mixed District Recorded by Supplier* [Dataset]. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11045013

Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The files are redistributed here unchanged, with attribution as the licence requires. Data provided by
Siemens AG for a study at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology.

| File | MD5 (matches Zenodo) | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `waterconsumption_rawdata.csv` | b223e715ae2bafb62970f2aef2fb45f2 | raw readings, 509 missing consumption values, 23/25-hour DST days |
| `waterconsumption_noNaN.csv` | 9e221170ebc84cdbc48dd3863b84d563 | gaps interpolated; 502-hour June 2017 gap filled with multi-year averages |
| `waterconsumption_noNaN_fixedTimechange.csv` | b894384bb148922507e1a96bc7f19beb | as above, plus every day has exactly 24 hours (**used for modelling**) |

Columns: `Date` (M/D/YYYY), `Time` (hour interval, e.g. `07:00-08:00`), `Consumption` (m³ per hour),
`Temperature` (°C), `Rain` and `Sun` (arbitrary units). Period: 1 Jan 2016 – 1 Apr 2021, hourly, one pumping
station in Germany serving residential, commercial and industrial users.

## `processed/` — generated by `src/data_preprocessing.py`

`water_hourly_clean.csv`: a regular hourly series indexed by `timestamp`, with flags marking every hour the
publisher imputed (`imputed_missing`), adjusted for daylight saving (`dst_adjusted`) or rounded
(`other_modified`), plus calendar features. No rows were removed.
